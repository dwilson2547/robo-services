## Phone gateway, discovery and data quality (2026-11-04)

- [ ] **Reject bad GPS fixes.** Drop or flag a fix whose reported accuracy, HDOP or satellite count is poor, or whose implied speed or jump from the previous fix is impossible. Do this in vehicle-state, or in the phone's FrameAssembler with the raw fix kept. The parking garage on 2026-11-04 produced junk fixes. With no dead-reckoning source accurate enough, a garage stays a gap: mark it, don't invent it.

- [ ] **Phone: drop back-to-back repeats.** Drop a frame identical (id, data, millisecond) to the previous one of its id, so a car like the Blazer still gives thin but clean data.

Extend the dongle's FILTER with a per-id maximum rate, chosen from discovery's inventory (rate, which bytes move).
